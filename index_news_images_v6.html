Based on team avg corners/match
Teams split by corner line thresholds using their season average. Each section shows which teams typically sit Over or Under that line — use this alongside live odds to spot value bets.

Fixture Corner Probabilities

Poisson model · live fixture data
Each live fixture is assessed using a Poisson distribution on the combined team corner average. Over / Under probabilities are shown for all four lines — 9.5 · 10.5 · 11.5 · 12.5 — in separate sections. The probability of not reaching the line (Under) is highlighted in red; of exceeding it (Over) in green.
